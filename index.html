How I work

The principles I apply to MEAL in any programme.

Fit for purpose

Keep MEAL proportionate to the programme, its modality and its risks, and build on tools that already work.

Quality at the source

Clear definitions, trained teams, spot checks, DQAs and verification against source documents before any figure is used or reported.

Safe and accountable

Feedback channels people can actually use; informed consent, minimal and anonymized personal data with controlled access; and sensitive and PSEA cases routed to safeguarding, never to routine reports.

Evidence that leads to action

Short, decision-ready findings for managers and donors that link results, challenges and adaptations.

Learning as routine

Learning reviews and After Action Reviews, with recommendations followed up rather than filed.

Capacity that stays

Train and coach staff and partners so that MEAL continues to work after I leave.
